Privacy

Privacy

VETO collects as little as it can. Here's exactly what's involved.

The website

  • No accounts, no tracking. The site has no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics scripts.
  • Your wallet address is read by the page in your browser when you connect, to show your vault. It isn't sent to us.
  • Saved in your browser: the demo's slider settings, so they're still there next time. Nothing else.
  • Services the page loads from: fonts from Google Fonts, the blockchain library from jsDelivr, and blockchain data from Robinhood Chain's public network. Those services receive the usual request details, such as your IP address.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs.

The Telegram bot

  • What it stores: which Telegram chat is linked to which vault address, and the orders you place (like stop-losses). It doesn't store your messages.
  • AI understanding (optional): if the bot's operator turns it on, your trade messages are sent to Anthropic's API to understand free-form requests. Otherwise messages are read only by the bot's built-in parser.
  • Telegram itself handles your messages under its own privacy policy.

The blockchain

Vaults, rules and trades are recorded on Robinhood Chain, which is public. Anyone can see on-chain activity for an address, and it can't be deleted.

Your choices

Send /unlink to the bot to remove your chat-to-vault link and cancel your open orders. Clear your browser's site data to remove the demo settings.
